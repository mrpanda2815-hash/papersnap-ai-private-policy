1. Introduction

Welcome to PaperSnap AI ("the Application", "the App", "we", "our", or "us"), developed and maintained by Manoranjan Panda.

Your privacy is paramount to us. PaperSnap AI is designed with a privacy-first, local-processing architecture. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the application accesses, how it is handled, and your rights and choices regarding your data when you install, scan, and use PaperSnap AI on Android devices.

Summary: PaperSnap AI processes documents directly on your device. We do not sell your personal data. Scanned documents, OCR recognition, cropped images, and custom folders remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or submit text to AI assistance features.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Document Scans & Images (Processed Locally)

When you use the document scanner (via Google Play Services ML Kit Document Scanner or Camera viewfinder) or import images from your gallery:

  • Camera Feed & Photo Capture: Used solely in real-time to detect document boundaries, crop, enhance, and create scanned pages.
  • On-Device Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Text recognition is performed on-device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition. The scanned text is saved to your device's private Room database (papersnap_v3_clean_database).
  • Local Storage: Scanned pages, PDFs, and custom category metadata are stored in your device's private application sandbox. They are never automatically uploaded to any third-party cloud server without your explicit command.

B. AI Document Assistant (Optional User-Triggered Processing)

PaperSnap AI includes an intelligent document assistant that provides summaries, plain-language legal/financial explanations, key data extraction (e.g. invoice numbers, amounts), and document Q&A.

  • On-Device Local Mode (Default): Document analysis runs locally on your device without sending text across the internet.
  • Cloud AI (Gemini via Firebase AI Logic): If you choose to enable Cloud AI for higher-reasoning queries, the extracted text from the selected document is sent securely over encrypted HTTPS to Google Gemini via Firebase AI Logic to generate answers. No permanent user profile is built from your document queries.

C. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

PaperSnap AI utilizes Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous data for ad personalization, frequency capping, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) / Device identifiers
  • Coarse location or IP address (for localized ad delivery)
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, impressions)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, carrier, network connection)

You can control personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by navigating to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

3. Device Permissions Used by PaperSnap AI

Permission Type Purpose
android.permission.CAMERA Runtime (Requested in UI) Allows live camera document scanning, automatic edge detection, and perspective alignment.
android.permission.INTERNET Normal (Install-time) Required for Google AdMob ad delivery and optional Firebase AI Logic (Gemini API) responses.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Verifies whether internet connectivity is active before loading ads or cloud AI features.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal (Install-time) Provides tactile haptic feedback when capturing frames or snapping crop boundaries.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Optional) Delivers smart reminders and due-date alerts scheduled for your bills and contracts.

Note on Storage: PaperSnap AI uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia / GetContent) and does not request broad legacy storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

5. Data Retention & User Control

Because your scanned documents and extracted OCR text are stored locally on your device:

  • Deletion: Deleting a page, document, or folder within the app immediately and permanently purges that data from your device storage.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling PaperSnap AI completely removes the application database, scanned bitmaps, generated PDFs, and user settings from your device.
  • Backup & Export: You maintain full ownership of your data and can export documents as searchable PDFs or JPEG images at any time through standard Android share sheets.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have statutory privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: View and export any documents saved within the application.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Delete any document or clean all app data directly via device App Settings.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Ad Tracking: Reset or delete your Google Advertising ID in device settings or choose non-personalized ads.

8. Security Safeguards

We employ industry-standard security practices to safeguard your information. Scanned documents are stored in the Android private internal app directory (inaccessible to other applications without root privileges). All external network communication (such as AdMob ad requests and AI queries) takes place exclusively over TLS/HTTPS encryption.
